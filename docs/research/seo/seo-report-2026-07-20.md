**Window:** 2026-06-18 → 2026-07-17 vs prior 30 days 2026-05-19 → 2026-06-17. (3-day data lag applied.)

|---|---|---|---|
| Clicks | 110 | 85 | +29% |

**The story:** visibility 2.5×'d, clicks barely moved, CTR halved, average position got *worse*. Google is now showing the site for far more searches — but at page 2–4, and mostly to the wrong audience. Empty visibility.

## The 6 insights that matter

1. **A third of the month's impressions were a one-day mirage.** July 2 alone = 9,005 impressions (21% of the whole month). That's a Google ranking test that didn't stick — impressions have declined since (~1,500/day early July → ~620/day now). The trend is cooling, not building.

2. **30% of all impressions go to two posts that earn ~2 clicks total.**
   - `/blog/n8n-ai-agents-features-2026-complete-guide` — 7,390 imp, 1 click, pos 7.6
   - `/blog/n8n-ai-agent-node-build-multi-agent-systems-in-2026` — 5,711 imp, 1 click, pos 22.1
   Combined 13,101 impressions (30% of site total) → 2 clicks. They rank for *"n8n ai agents official documentation 2026"* and similar. Those searchers want n8n's own docs — they will never click an agency blog. Vanity impressions inflating the 43K.

3. **The business is still branded search.** The query "chronexa" = 41 of 110 clicks (37%). The homepage earns 51 clicks at 6.4% CTR — by far the best converter. Strip out brand + homepage and the entire ~150-post blog earned roughly **59 clicks in 30 days — under 2 a day.**

4. **Geography is inverted from where the impressions are.** USA = 25,979 impressions but only 15 clicks (0.06% CTR — effectively invisible / wrong intent). India = 859 imp / 34 clicks / 3.96% (that's brand + team). UAE = 1,513 imp / 14 clicks / 0.93%. UAE converts best on real intent *and* matches the ICP.

5. **Desktop is where the junk lives.** Desktop 41,066 imp / 0.20% CTR vs Mobile 1,981 imp / 1.36% CTR. The huge desktop impression base is the low-intent doc/boolean-operator noise; mobile converts ~7× better.

6. **Winnable opportunities sit on page 2, and they're all UAE-commercial.**
   - "ai automation agency dubai" — pos 18.7
   - "ai automation uae" — pos 12.6
   - "hospital workflow automation uae" — pos 18.1
   Pages that actually earn clicks today: `top-ai-automation-agencies-b2b-saas-2026` (8), `blog-top-automation-agencies-uae-dubai` (7), `fintech-automation-with-n8n` (6), `seo-automation-with-n8n` (6).

## What I'd do next (SEO priorities)

1. **Don't celebrate the 43K.** Fix or accept the two n8n-docs posts — re-angle titles/metas to a decision/comparison intent you can actually win ("what it costs to build" not "official documentation"), or treat them as vanity and stop optimising for them.
2. **Push the UAE/Dubai commercial cluster from page 2 to page 1.** It already has the best non-brand CTR and matches the ICP. Internal links + a dedicated UAE/Dubai services page is the cheapest win on the board.
3. **CTR rescue on page-1 blog posts.** Rewrite title tags to mirror the actual query + a number/benefit. The homepage proves 6.4% CTR is achievable; the blog sits at 0.2–0.7%.
4. **The growth lever is authority, not more posts.** Non-brand discovery is flat — this matches the June forensic audit (authority problem, not on-page). Backlinks/mentions, not post volume.
