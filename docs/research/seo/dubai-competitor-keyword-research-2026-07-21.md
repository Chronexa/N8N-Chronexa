## Strategic caveat (important) — on-strategy vs traffic

Our doctrine (`blog-seo-strategy`) puts us in regulated/enterprise verticals: PE/M&A, wealth/RIA, law firms, CPA/tax, financial services — where data-sovereignty is a real differentiator. Much of Dubai's *easy* traffic (WhatsApp, real estate, SMB automation) is off that positioning and down-market.

**Recommended barbell:**
- **On-strategy core (defensible, converts our ICP):** finance/VAT/CFO automation UAE, legal automation UAE, wealth-management UAE (already ours), CPA/tax. These align with existing service pages and our differentiator.
- **Top-of-funnel grabs (traffic, selective):** the highest-value WhatsApp + real-estate Dubai terms — but each should still route to an n8n/enterprise automation payoff, not position us as an SMB WhatsApp shop.
