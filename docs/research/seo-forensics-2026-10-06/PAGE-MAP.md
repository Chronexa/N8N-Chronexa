Built from Ankit's positioning (2026-10-07) and checked against live search data (DataForSEO, US, today). Each page lists the search family it owns, monthly searches, cost-per-click (how much advertisers pay, a proxy for buyer value), and who holds page 1 now.

## The messaging spine (every page carries this)

**One line:** We're your AI integration partner. We audit how your company actually works, then build the intelligence and automations that let the same team serve 5–10× the clients.

**What makes us different (use these exact ideas everywhere):**
1. **We start with your business, not a template.** Every engagement starts by sitting with founders, auditing systems, workflows, tools and people, and picking the one lever that matters now.
2. **We don't build what your tools already do.** Every app now ships basic connectors and MCP integrations. We build what they can't: company knowledge that agents can read, so they act like an experienced employee, and review and approve work instead of just moving data.
3. **Knowledge first.** We centralise what your company knows (processes, past work, client history, rules) into systems agents can use. That's the difference between a "Zap" and a colleague.
4. **ROI is the lens.** Every build is sized on time saved, capacity gained and revenue unlocked. Example: if 10 people serve 100 clients a month today, what does it take to serve 500 with the same 10?
5. **We connect what you already use.** No forced stack changes. We build APIs and MCP servers for old tools that don't connect. If you want a modern stack, we recommend it, migrate your data and set it up.
6. **We ship in a week.** A first working automation inside a week, and infrastructure set up with it.
7. **Trust before money.** The audit is free. We build your first workflow free. Then we become your ongoing AI team, not a one-off vendor that hands over a workflow and leaves.
8. **We make your team AI-native.** We train people to use AI and automations properly.

**Pricing language:** "There's no one price, because no two companies are the same. The audit and first workflow are free. After that we price each build against the ROI it unlocks, agreed before we start." No conflicting numbers anywhere else on the site (fixes the $3,500 vs $8K–$80K vs $5K–$50K contradiction). Ankit to decide whether the $3,500 document pipeline stays as a separate fixed product.

**Claims to avoid:** "100× revenue" as a promise. Use it as the ambition ("our goal"), with ROI shown as worked, labelled examples.
