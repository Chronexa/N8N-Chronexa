# PARKED: Meta Ads strategy fork — pick this up in a dedicated session

**Parked:** 2026-08-26. **Status:** undecided, deliberately deferred. Automation work continues meanwhile.

## The decision to be made

Chronexa is currently **paying for one market while positioning for another**. That incoherence is
the root issue — not ad creative, not targeting tweaks, not follow-up speed.

**Fork A — US accounting/legal, document-operations, ~$3,500 engagements.**
The proven, differentiated capability. But Meta is a doubtful channel for it: the one US-targeted
campaign ran at **₹6,171 CPM vs ₹183–273 CPM** for Indian broad targeting — roughly 25–34x more
expensive. Learning whether it works at all needs ₹2–3 lakh, not ₹400/day.

**Fork B — Indian SMB operational automation.**
What actually responded. 167 leads at ₹102 blended. Real demand: 14 manufacturers, plus real
estate, education, agencies, asking for WhatsApp automation, lead management, payment reminders,
dispatch tracking. Lower ticket, higher volume, a different company than Fork A.

## Evidence behind the framing (all pulled live from the ad account 2026-08-22)

- **Total lifetime spend ₹16,909 (~$195)** across 14 months, 8 campaigns, 4 unrelated offers.
  No campaign ever had enough budget to exit Meta's learning phase. Every conclusion drawn from
  this data so far is noise, including "our campaigns failed."
- **₹102 blended CPL.** Against a $3,500 offer, affordable cost per *qualified* lead is roughly
  ₹2,500–3,700. Paying ₹102 means buying a different audience entirely. **In B2B, cheap leads are
  a warning sign, not a win.**
- **The biggest-spending campaigns had no interest targeting at all** — broad, age 18–65, Instant
  Form, lead-gen optimisation. That combination instructs Meta to find the cheapest humans who
  will tap a form. It did: 17 solo founders, 21 two-to-five-person shops, answers like
  "everything" and "I want my work automatic".
- **138 of 167 leads came from Instagram** stories/reels/explore.
- **Four different offers ran simultaneously**: ₹25,000 Shopify store builds, generic AI
  automation, law-firm automation, B2B outbound teardowns. Nothing compounded.
- **No quality signal ever reached Meta.** Website pixel unset, Conversions API token unused, so
  the algorithm only ever optimised for "person tapped form".

## What must be decided before spending again

1. Which fork. Everything else follows.
2. One offer, one nameable audience.
3. Whether Meta is even the right channel for the chosen fork.
4. Budget sized to actually learn (~₹50,000 over 30 days on ONE campaign, not ₹400/day across four).
5. Quality signal fed back via the Conversions API so Meta optimises for buyers, not form-tappers.
